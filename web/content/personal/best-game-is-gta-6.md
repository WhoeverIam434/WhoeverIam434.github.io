# Best Game is GTA 6

This is a personal archive, so here is a personal take: the best game is the one
that gives you a world you can waste an afternoon in without noticing.

## The argument, briefly

- Systems that keep producing situations nobody scripted
- A city that feels like it was lived in, not just modelled
- Enough friction that the good moments are not handed to you

## The counterargument

Some people want a game they can finish. That is a different hobby, and it is
also valid.

> The best game is the one you are still thinking about three days later.

## What this has to do with robotics

Nothing. That is the point of an archive called "Personal Stuffs".
