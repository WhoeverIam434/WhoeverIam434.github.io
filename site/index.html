About关于我

I write firmware that makes machines move — and control loops that make them move well. 我编写让机器动起来的固件，也设计让它们动得更好的控制回路。

At HKUST's Robotics Team I develop embedded firmware in C for STM32F407 microcontrollers, covering sensor interfaces and motor control, while coordinating with mechanical and hardware subteams through Git-based workflows. My work on the War Dragon robot's climbing mechanism removed a motor overheating problem and an angle-rounding error, lifting climbing speed by more than 121%. 在香港科技大学机器人队，我用 C 语言为 STM32F407 开发嵌入式固件，涵盖传感器接口与电机控制，并通过 Git 工作流与机械、硬件小组协作。在 War Dragon 机器人爬升机构的研发中，我解决了电机过热与角度取整误差问题，使爬升速度提升超过 121%。

On the control side I built trapezoidal velocity profiles for a rack-and-pinion lift, delegated inner-loop motor control to VESC's position PID with FOC, and fused a 6-axis IMU through a Madgwick filter to localize the robot. I'm now researching magnetic tactile sensors at Mevita Robotics Lab. 在控制方面，我为齿轮齿条升降机构实现梯形速度规划，将内环电机控制交由 VESC 的位置 PID 与 FOC 处理，并用 Madgwick 滤波融合六轴 IMU 完成机器人定位。目前我在 Mevita Robotics Lab 研究磁性触觉传感器。
